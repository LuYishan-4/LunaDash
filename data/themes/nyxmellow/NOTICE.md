# NyxMellow attribution

The theme.conf, panel.svg and highlight.svg templates are adapted from
[ech678/NyxNiri](https://github.com/ech678/NyxNiri), commit
`adf0954c877d2c70de12a51b9fd6727a59352f98`, under GPL-3.0.
Upstream credits [fcitx5-mellow-themes](https://github.com/sanweiya/fcitx5-mellow-themes)
for the shape and [Round-Simple-Fcitx5-Skin](https://github.com/StarWhiteIsBusy/Round-Simple-Fcitx5-Skin)
for palette synchronization. The full upstream license is included.

LunaDash changes the displayed name, removes inline localized names, and renders
palette placeholders through its C++ theme backend instead of Noctalia.
The skin installs under `lunadash-mellow` after explicit opt-in.
